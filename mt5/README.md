# Tbot Adaptive EA for Deriv MT5

## A straight word on the "90% win rate"

No bot reliably wins 90% of its trades. Bots that show 90% wins usually get there with tiny take-profits and huge (or no) stop losses, so one bad trade wipes out weeks of gains. What matters is **expectancy**: win rate × average win − loss rate × average loss. This EA aims for that. It risks a fixed small % per trade, takes trades only when the market regime suits its logic, and stops itself on bad days. Expect a win rate somewhere around 40–60% depending on settings and symbol. It is profitable only if the winners are bigger than the losers, and **past backtest results do not guarantee future profit**.

Deriv's synthetic indices (Volatility 75 etc.) are generated by a random number generator. Patterns in them can disappear, so test carefully.

## What the bot does

| Step | Logic |
|---|---|
| Regime | ADX ≥ 25 means **trend**, ADX ≤ 20 means **range**, and in between it does not trade |
| Bias | Trend trades only in the direction of the higher-timeframe EMA (default H1 EMA 50) and its slope |
| Trend entry | Price pulls back to the fast EMA (21), closes back on the trend side with a confirming candle, +DI/−DI agree, RSI 50–70 (buys) or 30–50 (sells) |
| Range entry | Close back inside the Bollinger Band after an RSI extreme (<30 / >70), never against a clear higher-TF trend |
| Exits | ATR stop loss and take profit (trend 1.5 / 3.0 ATR, range 1.2 / 1.5 ATR), break-even at 1R, ATR trailing stop |
| Protection | 1% risk per trade, 3% daily loss limit (closes everything and stops for the day), max 6 trades/day, max 1 open trade, 8-bar cooldown after 3 losses in a row, spread and volatility-spike filters |

Every number above is an input you can change.

## Signals only or auto trade

The first input, **Mode**, picks how the bot works:

- **Signals only** (default): the bot never places a trade. When it finds a setup it draws an arrow plus dashed SL (red) and TP (green) lines on the chart. It also shows a pop-up alert with sound in MT5 and sends a push notification to the MT5 app on your phone. Each signal includes the entry, stop loss, take profit and a suggested lot size for your risk %. You decide whether to take it.
- **Auto trade**: the bot places, manages (break-even, trailing) and closes trades itself on the account the terminal is logged into.

To get signals on your phone: install the **MetaTrader 5** app, open *Settings → Messages* (Chat and messages) to find your **MetaQuotes ID**, then in desktop MT5 go to **Tools → Options → Notifications**, tick *Enable Push Notifications*, paste the ID and press *Test*.

Backtests need **Auto trade**, because signals-only mode places no trades and the Strategy Tester would show nothing.

## Connecting the bot to your Deriv account

The bot trades whatever account your MT5 terminal is logged into. You never give your password to the bot, to me, or to anyone else.

1. In the Deriv dashboard (app.deriv.com → *Trader's Hub*), create an **MT5 account**. Start with a **Demo** account. Choose *Derived* for synthetic indices or *Financial* for forex.
2. Note its MT5 **login number** and **server** (e.g. `Deriv-Demo`), and set its MT5 password there.
3. In desktop MT5: **File → Login to Trade Account**, enter the login, password and server.
4. Open a chart of the symbol, drag the EA onto it, choose the Mode, tick **Allow Algo Trading**, and make sure the **Algo Trading** toolbar button is green (only needed for Auto trade).
5. Moving to real money later means logging the terminal into your *real* Deriv MT5 account the same way. Do it only after a demo run you're happy with, and at low risk.

## 1. Install

1. Open your Deriv MT5 terminal (desktop version for Windows; the mobile app cannot run EAs).
2. **File → Open Data Folder**, then go to `MQL5\Experts\`.
3. Copy `TbotAdaptive.mq5` into that folder.
4. In MT5 press **F4** to open MetaEditor, open the file, and press **F7 (Compile)**. It should say `0 errors`. If it shows an error, send me the message and line number. (I couldn't compile it here because MetaEditor only runs inside MT5.)
5. Back in MT5, right-click **Expert Advisors** in the Navigator and choose **Refresh**. "TbotAdaptive" appears.

## 2. Backtest in the Strategy Tester

1. **View → Strategy Tester** (Ctrl+R).
2. Expert: `TbotAdaptive`. Symbol: e.g. `Volatility 75 Index` or `EURUSD`. Timeframe: M15 (matches the default entry TF).
3. Modelling: **Every tick based on real ticks** (most realistic). Use at least 1–2 years of data.
4. Deposit: what you'd actually trade with. Leverage: your account's.
5. In the Inputs tab set **Mode = Auto trade**. Run, then check the **Backtest** tab. Look at:
   - **Profit factor**: above 1.3 is decent, below 1.1 is noise.
   - **Equity drawdown (relative)**: should be something you could live through.
   - **Total trades**: under ~100 is too few to trust.
   - **Expected payoff**: positive.
6. **Optimising:** in the Inputs tab tick a few parameters (e.g. ADX levels, SL/TP ATR multiples) and choose *Custom max* as the criterion. The EA scores runs by profit factor, number of trades and drawdown rather than raw win rate.
7. **Avoid overfitting:** optimise on one period (e.g. 2023–2024), then run the best settings unchanged on a later period (e.g. 2025). Use MT5's *Forward* option (1/3) to do this automatically. If results collapse on the forward period, the settings were curve-fitted.

## 3. Demo first

1. Open a **Deriv demo MT5 account** and log in to it in MT5.
2. Drag `TbotAdaptive` onto a chart of the symbol (any timeframe; the EA uses its own TF inputs).
3. Set **Mode** (signals only or auto trade). For auto trade, tick **Allow Algo Trading** in the dialog and make sure the **Algo Trading** button in the toolbar is green.
4. The top-left panel shows status, regime, today's P/L, trades and loss streak. The **Experts** tab logs every decision and order.
5. Run it for at least **4–8 weeks** and compare with the backtest. Only consider a live account if demo results are in line, and then start with the minimum risk (e.g. `Risk per trade = 0.5`).
6. Running on several symbols: use a **different Magic number on each chart**.
7. The EA only trades while the terminal is running. For 24/7 synthetics, use a VPS.

## Live dashboard and running 24/7

- **Dashboard:** both bots can send every signal and trade to your own web page on Vercel, which you can check from your phone. It's the `/mt5/` page of the Tbot site. Setup is below. Set *Dashboard address* and *Secret* in the bot inputs. Leave them empty to turn this off.
- **MT5 must stay running.** The bot lives inside your MT5 terminal. If the terminal or PC is closed, it stops trading and signalling. Vercel only displays what the bot sends and cannot run the bot itself.
- **24/7:** MT5's built-in **virtual hosting** (Navigator → right-click your account → *Register a Virtual Server*) runs your terminal and bots on a MetaQuotes server around the clock. It's a paid monthly service. Migrate again after changing any settings. Details are below.

## Settings worth knowing

- **Risk per trade (%)**: the most important setting. 0.5–1% is sensible, and the EA refuses values above 10.
- **Daily loss limit (%)**: when equity falls this far below the day's starting balance, all positions close and trading stops until the next day.
- **Use trend / Use range**: you can switch either strategy off. On strongly trending synthetics you may find trend-only does better.
- **Max spread as fraction of ATR**: blocks entries when spread is large relative to current volatility.
- If you see "Skip: min lot … would risk …" in the log, your balance is too small for that symbol's minimum lot at your risk %. Choose a smaller symbol (e.g. Volatility 10/25) or a larger balance. Don't raise the risk.

Trading carries real risk of loss. Use money you can afford to lose.

## MT5 dashboard setup (optional)

The Tbot site on Vercel includes a page at `/mt5/` that shows these bots' signals and trades.

1. In the Vercel project, under **Settings → Environment Variables**, add `TBOT_SECRET`, a long random string. Optionally add `TBOT_VIEW_KEY` to make the page private (open it once as `/mt5/?key=...`).
2. Under **Storage**, add **Upstash for Redis** (free) and connect it, so history survives restarts. Then redeploy.
3. In MT5: **Tools → Options → Expert Advisors**, tick *Allow WebRequest for listed URL* and add your site address.
4. In each bot's inputs, set *Dashboard address* to the site address and *Secret* to `TBOT_SECRET`.

If you see "error 4014" in the Experts tab, the URL isn't allowed yet. "Dashboard replied 401" means the secret doesn't match.

**Keeping MT5 running 24/7:** the bots work only while the terminal is running. MT5's built-in virtual hosting keeps it running around the clock: in the Navigator, right-click your account → *Register a Virtual Server*. It's a paid MetaQuotes service. Migrate again after any change. On the virtual server, pop-up alerts won't reach you, so use phone notifications and the dashboard.
