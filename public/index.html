Login not working?
  • Your app's redirect URL at developers.deriv.com must be exactly . If you registered it without the slash at the end, tick that option under Settings, Deriv app.
  • Use the App ID of an app of the OAuth type, with the trading permission (trade) ticked.
  • Open this page in Chrome or Safari, not inside another app, and finish the login in the same browser.
