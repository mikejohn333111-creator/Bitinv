      Deriv connection

      How to get a token

      1. Open the Deriv developer dashboard at developers.deriv.com and log in with your Deriv account.
      2. If you have no app yet, register one. Copy its App ID.
      3. Create a Personal Access Token and tick the Trade permission. Copy the token.
      4. Paste the App ID and the token above, then tap Save and connect.

      The names on Deriv's site may be a little different. Look for "token" or "API token".
